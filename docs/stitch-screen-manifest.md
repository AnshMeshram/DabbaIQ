## Duplicate & Editorial Resolution Notes

### 1. Dual Recipe Library Screens
- Screen `22576da8e5654a12bebde9b6eed7b95d` (*Inside the Dabba — Recipe Library*) is an **editorial story canvas** highlighting the culinary heritage and BOM composition of heritage dabba dishes (Toor Dal, Basmati Jeera Rice, Phulkas, Aloo Gobi Adraki).
- Screen `27e0f6b927ed402ab8a28f54c52589c5` (*DabbaIQ — Recipe Library & Recipe Costing*) is the **operational production desk** featuring the live portion costing calculation (₹/portion), margin percentages, raw ingredient price linkage, and multilingual AI storytelling via Gemma 2.
- **Resolution**: Both screens are unified into the `/recipes` page and the `/experience` tier-3 dossier. Operators can inspect the full costing matrix while accessing the heritage BOM and culinary notes seamlessly without losing context.

### 2. Dual Inventory, Sales & Waste Screens
- Stitch contains paired "Inside the Dabba" editorial screens (`c40c8e...`, `5283dd...`, `ed7133...`) alongside operational desk screens (`26090f...`, `db3f41...`, `96503c...`).
- **Resolution**: The editorial narrative summaries are incorporated as high-context station banners and KPI header blocks at the top of each operational route (`/inventory`, `/sales`, `/waste`), while the full operational ledgers provide real-time transaction recording, filtering, and export.
